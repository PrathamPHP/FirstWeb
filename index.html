College Introduction

Welcome to our college Green Campus website. Our college encourages students to protect nature and keep the surroundings clean and green.

We believe that small steps like planting trees, saving electricity and reducing plastic can make a big difference.

Green Campus Activities

Students and teachers take part in different activities to help the environment.

  • Tree planting programmes
  • Campus cleaning drives
  • Say no to plastic campaigns
  • Water saving awareness programmes
  • Recycling paper and other materials

Solar Energy

Solar energy is a clean and renewable source of energy. Solar panels use sunlight to produce electricity.

Using solar energy can help our college save electricity and reduce pollution.

Waste Management

Proper waste management is important for a clean campus. We should separate waste into different dustbins.

  • Green bin: Wet waste like food and leaves
  • Blue bin: Dry waste like paper and plastic

We should always use dustbins and avoid littering.
